# What to ask when the brief is silent

Ask only what the brief leaves open; at most ten, numbered, each with your
default. Defaults used for surf_100_leagues are in brackets.

1. Name and tier. [as given; tier 1]
2. Linear or staged? [linear, with drop-in buoys]
3. How long is a good run? [the brief's number becomes gold]
4. What happens when you fall? [back to the last buoy, stopped, clock on]
5. How does it end? [a finish zone where the brief lands you]
6. Each thing's juggle, if left blank. [tossed hand to hand]
7. Each thing's inspect, if left blank. [turned over in the hands]
8. How is the costume earned, and where is it worn? [finish the course;
   a setting; others and your ghost see it, your own view does not]
9. A sentence that stops short or could mean two things (quote it).
10. Deploy when the checks pass, or wait to play first? [as the brief says]
