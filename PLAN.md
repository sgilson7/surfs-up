# Plan

Stage 2, from `BRIEF.md`. The brief approved the plan in advance ("don't stop till it's deployed"), so each decision below was taken, built and recorded rather than held for a reading; the ones that stay with the person are in `SECOND-ORDER.md` as worklist rows.

## Decisions

**P1. The map is the real surf_utopia, not a sketch of it.** The brief asks for surf_utopia "perfectly recreated". `tools/extract_map.py` reads Panzerhandschuh's `surf_utopia_v3.bsp` (GameBanana) and keeps its brushes exactly: every plane of every solid the Source engine collides a player against, bevel planes included, plus the trigger volumes and the start. Collision then runs against the same convex brushes Source uses, so a ramp's angle, a seam or a gap is where it was. The TF2 jail below the course (z < -8250) is dropped; the course is the same one CS:S and CS:GO players knew (CS:GO's version is the jail-less `surf_utopia_njv`). No texture is used.

**P2. Movement is Source's, in floats.** `crates/core/src/movement.rs` follows Source SDK 2013's CGameMovement function by function: FullWalkMove's order (half gravity, jump, friction, move, categorise, half gravity), AirAccelerate with the 30-unit wish cap that is compared against but not used for the rate, ClipVelocity with overbounce 1, TryPlayerMove's bumps and creases, the 0.7 ground normal, the 140 u/s non-jump velocity, StepMove, StayOnGround, TryTouchGroundInQuadrants, and the 0.25 surface friction while rising. Constants are CS:GO surf-server values (`TUNING`; sources in `docs/RESEARCH-surf-movement.md`): 64 tick, gravity 800, airaccelerate 150, accelerate 10, friction 5.2, stopspeed 80, 250 wish speed, maxvelocity 3500 per axis, autobhop on. This changes reference default D2 (no floats): see `BUILDER-SPEC.md`.

**P3. Rampbugs are fixed, as surf servers fix them.** The research recommends shipping the mitigations from day one: eight bumps a tick, a plane hit twice in one tick is clipped again with a 0.25 u/s push-off instead of making a zero-length crease, no velocity zeroing for "could not move" in the air, a stuck box nudged out (CheckStuck), slope landings that take the better of clip and snap, and triggers tested along the whole swept box (no telehops or missed thin triggers). `the_first_ridge_has_no_rampbug_on_either_side` holds the first ridge's seam, which froze a scripted surfer before the push-off.

**P4. A run.** The clock starts when you leave the start platform (prespeed capped at 290 u/s, shavit's default) and stops at one of the three doors on the final wall (the original's `jail_control_room1`, `jail_bottom1`, `jail_center` endings). The three earlier endings are early exits and do not time a run. Falling into a pit (the original's `jail1` teleports) or a green door (`start`) sends you back to the start; so does R. Sync, strafes and gain are counted the way shavit's bhoptimer and SSJ count them.

**P5. Hands and what they hold.** You start with your palms pressed together. Finishing earns a pool noodle; bronze, silver and gold times earn a red kickball, a squeaky duck and a foam finger; holding the best time in the gym (the gym record, or the room's best online) earns the rubber chicken. The mouse button squeezes what you hold, and the others hear it. Medal times are provisional (`game.rs`: 1:20 gym record, 1:40 gold, 2:10 silver, 3:00 bronze) until people have played the course.

**P6. Online: ptonkle's mesh, rollback per surfer, ghosts once.** The transport is ptonkle's Ape Race `online.js` unchanged but for its header: a full WebRTC mesh, introduced by a room code over public Nostr relays (Trystero) or by pasted codes with no relay. The core decides what is said:
- Live surfers: inputs two ticks at a time (about five bytes a tick) and the exact state twice a second. Each browser runs every other surfer through the same `Surfer::step`, predicts unheard ticks by repeating the last input, and rolls back to the confirmed state when the inputs arrive. Surfers never collide, so a rollback is one surfer's, never a cascade. Small corrections are eased out of the drawn position.
- Ghosts: a finished run (its start state and inputs) is sent once, whole, and a newcomer is sent your best on arrival. The next run you start, every run you hold replays beside you entirely in your own browser.
- "Show other surfers live" can be turned off; then only finished runs travel.
The room holds eight. `tests/browser/online.py` puts eight browsers in one room.

**P7. The look: a school gym.** Ramps are gym mats (one colour per connected ramp, stitched panels), floors are maple boards, walls are painted cinderblock with a blue and gold stripe, glass is safety netting, pits are full of foam cubes, finish doors are checkered, doors back to the start are green, and the space around the course is a gymnasium with high windows and pennants. Flat-shaded and low-poly with haze, after Ape Race. 2D art is TikZ (`tools/art.py`). The name is "Surfs Up!"; the one Counter-Strike nod is the coach's tip about rushing B(leachers).

**P8. Deploy.** A public repository `sgilson7/surfs-up`, GitHub Pages from `gh-pages` (the built `dist/`), and a listing on sgilson7.github.io in the way Raiders of the Lost Mart and Level Twenty were listed. The brief gives approval for all three.

**P9. A second course, surf_kitsune (added 13 October 2026, from Sam's second request: "can you implement surf kitsune, with the same gym style as utopia got?").** Arblarg's surf_kitsune (CS:S, GameBanana) is read by the same extractor, now writing map format v2: teleport destinations, the activator-name filters its secrets use, and the triggers that rename you. What each teleport means moved out of the code into `data/maps/maps.json` (`crates/core/src/course.rs`): utopia's falls and green doors restart and its three bottom doors finish; kitsune's nine stages start at red, orange, yellow, green, teal, blue, purple, pink and white, a fall returns you to your stage's start with the clock running, the white stage's end door finishes, and the secret bonus room's pit restarts. The surfer carries the name the map gave it, so a filtered shortcut fires only for someone who found the secret, as on a server. Bests, ghosts, medal times and the scoreboard are per course; things to hold are earned on any course against that course's medals; online, you see live only the surfers on your course. The title picks the course, and the page fetches its map file when chosen. The gym look applies unchanged, with two rules made general: a see-through ramp is a mat and a see-through floor is maple (kitsune builds its ramps from glass grids), and tool-black surfaces are painted like any other (kitsune walls its void in black).
